AI ADOPTION / FIELD NOTES

Start with the problem.
Then explore the AI.

Read perspective +

Choose a task with a clear owner, a repeatable workflow and a measurable outcome. Establish how it works today before introducing AI.

Test on representative examples. Review errors as carefully as successes, and decide where a person must approve the result. A small, well-defined pilot can reveal whether a larger investment makes sense.

TECHNOLOGY STRATEGY / FIELD NOTES

A roadmap is a series
of better decisions.

Read perspective +

Begin with business priorities and the constraints around them. Map the systems, dependencies and decisions that stand between the current state and the desired outcome.

Sequence work around value and risk. Give each stage a clear purpose, an owner and a way to learn. Revisit the plan as evidence changes rather than treating the first version as a fixed promise.

HUMAN-CENTRED DESIGN / FIELD NOTES

Adoption starts
before launch.

Read perspective +

Invite the people who will use a system into the design process early. Observe their current work and ask where effort, confusion or unnecessary steps accumulate.

Make room for accessibility, training and feedback. A technically complete system still needs to fit into daily work. Success measures should reflect that experience, alongside technical performance.
